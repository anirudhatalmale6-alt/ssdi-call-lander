You may have a claim if…

Most people who qualify meet all of the following. If that sounds like you, it is worth a call.

  • A medical condition has kept you out of work for 12 months, or is expected to
  • You are between 18 and 65
  • You have worked and paid into Social Security in recent years
  • You are being treated by a doctor for the condition
  • You are not already receiving disability benefits

Already been denied? That is normal — most first applications are turned down. A denial does not end your claim, and appeals are often where cases are won. It is still worth calling.
